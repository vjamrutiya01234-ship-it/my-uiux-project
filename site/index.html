FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Lumen change anything in my other tools?

No. Integrations are read-only by default. Lumen only writes focus blocks to your calendar if you turn that on.

How long does setup take?

About five minutes: connect a calendar, connect one task tool, and choose your three outcomes for the week.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel anytime from settings. Yearly plans are prorated.

Is my data used to train AI models?

Never. Your plans and connected data are used only to provide the service to your workspace.
